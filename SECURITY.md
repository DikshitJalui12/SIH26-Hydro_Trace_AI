## Reporting a Vulnerability

The HydroTrace-AI engineering team takes cyber-hardening and DevSecOps seriously:

1. **Private Reporting**: If you discover a security vulnerability or cryptographic integrity bypass, please do NOT create a public issue.
2. **Contact**: Submit vulnerability details to the designated security contact or email `security@hydrotrace-ai.org`.
3. **Response Time**: We acknowledge receipt of reports within 48 hours and provide a remediation timeline.
4. **Adherence**: All court-admissible audit records are chained via HMAC-SHA256 digests and subject to strict verification under MARPOL Annex I legal standards.
